# Project: น้ำเหนือรีสอร์ท บางเสร่ (Nam Nuea Resort, Bang Saray)

## Brand
- Resort in Bang Saray, Sattahip, Chon Buri, Thailand. Budget-friendly (rooms in the hundreds of baht), clean, safe, warm, close to Sattahip and Bang Saray check-in spots.
- Brand feeling: "Feel at บ้าน (home) in Bang Saray" — warm, welcoming, relaxed, honest.
- Audience: Gen Z and millennial travelers (18-35), mobile-first, Thai and international. They judge a site in 3 seconds on their phone, share on Instagram/TikTok, and book via LINE or direct message.

## Design Language: "Warm Minimal"
- Calm, airy, premium-feeling but approachable. Quiet luxury with a human, handwritten touch.
- Colors: warm off-white #F7F4EF, sand #E8E0D4, surface #EFE9E0, sage #8A9A85, text #2B2724, muted #6F6862. One warm accent allowed (soft amber/terracotta) for CTAs and highlights.
- Type: Thai headings/body in a clean modern Thai font (current site font); Latin accents in Cormorant Garamond or Jost; handwritten Thai accent (Mali / Itim / Sriracha) for ONE highlight word per section only.
- Radius 2-4px, hairline borders, very subtle shadows, generous whitespace, 8px spacing system.

## Signature Elements (keep)
- Hero tagline "Feel at [บ้าน ↔ Home] in Bang Saray": the word loops every 4000ms, handwritten, with a hand-drawn underline. Do not change the words or copy.
- Thai headline "พักผ่อนอย่างสบายใจ ในบรรยากาศที่แสนอบอุ่น" stays as is.

## Hard Rules
- Never change business content: prices, room names, phone/LINE, addresses, policies, routes, forms, booking logic.
- Mobile-first. Test 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1440px. No horizontal scroll. Tap targets >= 44px. Use 100svh/dvh, not 100vh.
- Thai text: line-height >= 1.6, never clip vowels/tone marks, use text-wrap: balance for headings.
- Animate only transform and opacity where possible. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No bounce, no flashy effects.
- WCAG AA contrast. Semantic HTML. Real alt text. Lazy-load images below the fold.
- Keep dependencies minimal. Prefer plain CSS + small JS. Ask before adding a library.
- Never invent reviews, awards, prices, or photos. Use placeholders clearly marked TODO.
- Work in small steps; after each step run the dev server, take screenshots, check the console, and fix before moving on.
